His work spans live news coverage, editorial storytelling, and the full photo workflow from field capture through Photoshop editing to submission under deadline pressure — extending to international assignments, including freelance work for CBC Canada, and long-running contributions to Punjab Kesari and the Press Trust of India (PTI).
